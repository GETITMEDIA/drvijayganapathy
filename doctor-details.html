Biography

A Dedicated Specialist in Minimally Invasive Urology

Dr. S. Vijay Ganapathy is a senior Consultant Urologist, Andrologist, and Kidney Stone Specialist practising in Puducherry. He completed his MBBS, MS (General Surgery) at JIPMER, DNB (Surgery), and MCh (Urology) as a Gold Medalist, followed by international fellowships in Endourology & Andrology (Singapore) and Urogynaecology (Italy).

With over a decade of clinical and surgical experience, he focuses on stitchless laser kidney stone removal (RIRS & Mini-PCNL), prostate care, male infertility & microsurgery, paediatric urology, and female urogynaecology.
