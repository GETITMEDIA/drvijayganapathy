Biography

Academic Journey & Clinical Dedication

I am Dr. Vijay Ganapathy, a native of the French city of Pondicherry. After a memorable schooling in Petit Seminaire High School, I went on to study Medicine at Pondicherry Institute of Medical Sciences, Pondicherry University.

I furthered my education with a Master's in Surgery at Jawaharlal Institute of Postgraduate Medical Education and Research (JIPMER), Puducherry, India — graduating as the topper with a gold medal.

I completed my Urological Residency (MCh) at the Institute of Nephrourology, Bengaluru, a dedicated center of excellence performing 500–600 urological surgeries monthly across laparoscopy, endourology, and renal transplantation, and later completed my Andrology Fellowship sub-specialization at Singapore General Hospital and NUHS, Singapore.

My research in Urodynamics earned the Urogynaecology Fellowship from the International Continence Society (ICS 2017) in Florence, Italy, supported by an ICMR travel grant, and I presented a randomized controlled study on T. Pregabalin's efficacy in stent removal at the World Congress of Endourology (Paris, 2018).

Clinical Focus

Field of Expertise & Research

As a practicing urologist and andrologist, my primary areas of care are minimally invasive management for kidney stone diseases through scarless and keyhole procedures (ESWL, PCNL, Mini-Perc, RIRS, URSL), prostate laser care, and vascular access for kidney failure patients.

I am deeply passionate about Men's Health Management—breaking cultural taboos around male fertility, sexual wellness, and preventive urology so every patient receives dignified, confidential, evidence-based care.
