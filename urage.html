85–90% Treatable Conditions

Restoring Comfort & Bladder Control

There is no reason to suffer silently from a pelvic or bladder condition. Nearly 85% to 90% of urogynecologic conditions are successfully treated through targeted lifestyle modifications, pelvic floor rehabilitation, medications, or brief minimally invasive procedures.

Urogynaecology addresses urinary incontinence, pelvic organ prolapse, urodynamic bladder dysfunction, and childbirth-related pelvic floor strain.

Condition Focus

1. Urinary Stress Incontinence

Urinary stress incontinence occurs when urine leaks during everyday physical activities such as coughing, sneezing, laughing, or exercise. A healthy pelvic floor acts like a supportive sling beneath the urethra; when weakened or stretched, physical pressure briefly breaks that seal.

Accurate urodynamic evaluation helps distinguish stress incontinence from urge symptoms so the gentlest effective care is chosen.

Minimally Invasive Sling

2. Transobturator Tape (TOT) Procedure

The TOT procedure supports the urethra using a soft permanent mesh sling placed through tiny incisions under anesthesia. Acting like a natural hammock beneath the urethra during coughing or exertion, TOT restores bladder control with minimal discomfort and fast recovery.

30–45 Minute Repair

3. Transvaginal Tape (TVT) Repair

TVT is a globally trusted, tension-free support technique that reinforces the urethra only when abdominal pressure increases. Taking just 30–45 minutes, it delivers high long-term continence rates with shorter hospital stays and rapid return to daily life.
