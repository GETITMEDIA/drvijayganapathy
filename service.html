We Offer Different Services To Improve Your Health

Dr. Vijay Ganapathy, a highly experienced and qualified doctor, offers a variety of services to improve your health. He is a gold medalist in M.S (JIPMER), D.N.B (Surgery), M.Ch (Urology), and has received fellowships in USI Endourology (SGH-Singapore) and ICS Urogynaecology (Florence, Italy).

Kidney Stone (சிறுநீரக கல்)

Dr. Ganapathy offers expert care for kidney stones, utilizing the latest techniques in urology to treat and manage kidney-related issues.

Urogynaecology
(சிறுநீரக சிகிச்சை)

Specialized treatment for urological cancers, with advanced methods to diagnose and treat urinary tract cancers effectively.

Male Infertility (ஆண் கருவுறாமை)

Dr. Ganapathy offers comprehensive treatment for male infertility, including diagnosis and therapy tailored to individual needs.

Prostate Problems (புரோஸ்டேட் பிரச்சனை)

Effective treatments for prostate issues, including both medical and surgical options for prostate enlargement and related concerns.

Pediatric Urology (குழந்தை சிறுநீரக சிகிச்சை)

Specialized care for children's urological problems, offering safe and effective treatments for various pediatric urological conditions.

Andrology (ஆண் உறுப்பு நோயியல்)

A specialized approach to male reproductive health, including the treatment of erectile dysfunction, low testosterone, and other andrological disorders.
