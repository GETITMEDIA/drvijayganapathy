Discreet, Evidence-Based Men's Health

Our Andrology Clinic provides a calm, strictly confidential environment where local and visiting patients can speak openly about male sexual wellness, fertility, and uro-genital conditions.

Led by Dr. S. Vijay Ganapathy—who completed sub-specialty Andrology fellowships at Singapore General Hospital and NUHS, Singapore—we treat conditions ranging from erectile dysfunction, premature or delayed ejaculation, and low libido to Peyronie's disease, penile curvature, varicocele, and scrotal pain.

Every care plan begins with accurate physiological and hormonal assessment, offering medically validated therapies, rehabilitation programs, and surgical options tailored to restore confidence and quality of life.

Clinical Scope

Conditions Treated in Men's Health

  • Erectile problems and performance anxiety
  • Ejaculatory issues & premature emission
  • Penile swelling, wound, or discomfort
  • Penile curvature & Peyronie's disease
  • Scrotal pain & swelling
  • Orgasm disorders & low libido
  • Marital & couple sexual wellness concerns
Diagnostics & Therapies

Facilities & Procedures Provided

  • Men's sexual health diagnostic assessment
  • Comprehensive hormonal analysis
  • High-resolution Penile Doppler ultrasound
  • Coital counseling and therapeutic rehabilitation
  • PDE5 inhibitor & intracavernosal injection therapy
  • Medically approved vacuum constriction & extension devices
  • Penile revascularisation & penile prosthesis surgery
  • Testosterone replacement therapy
