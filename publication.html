Publications

Publications in Indexed Journals

Penile circular fasciocutaneous (McAninch) flap as an option for complex anterior urethral stricture

Authors: Vijay Ganapathy S, Karthikeyan VS, Mallya A, Vishwanath K, Sreenivas J, Keshavamurthy R

Journal: J Clin Diagn Res. 2016 Jul;10(7):PD09-11

Details: This publication discusses the use of penile circular fasciocutaneous (McAninch) flap as an alternative for complex anterior urethral stricture in cases where the buccal mucosal graft is not viable.

Page kidney in Wunderlich syndrome causing acute renal failure and urosepsis

Authors: Vijay Ganapathy S, Sreenivas J, Keshavamurthy R

Journal: J Clin Diagn Res. 2017 Jul;15(8):24731

Details: A case study on the management of page kidney in Wunderlich syndrome, which led to acute renal failure and urosepsis. The article highlights successful minimally invasive management techniques for this condition.

Nasogastric tube as abdominal pressure sensor in urodynamics

Authors: Ganapathy SV, Keshavamurthy R, Poonawala A, Mallya A, Karthikeyan VS

Journal: International Journal of Urology. 2016 Jul, Vol. 23, 111 RIVER ST, HOBOKEN 07030-5774, NJ USA: WILEY-BLACKWELL

Details: This article presents a novel approach using a nasogastric tube as an abdominal pressure sensor during urodynamics, offering a new method for improving diagnostic accuracy.

Validation of APACHE II scoring system at 24 hours after admission as a prognostic tool in urosepsis

Authors: Vijay Ganapathy S, Karthikeyan VS, Mallya A

Journal: Investigative and Clinical Urology. Accepted in June 2017, Manuscript ID: ICU-17-050

Details: This publication validates the APACHE II scoring system as a reliable prognostic tool for evaluating urosepsis patients within 24 hours of admission.

Nasogastric tube as abdominal pressure sensor in urodynamics: Proof of concept

Authors: Vijay Ganapathy S, Karthikeyan VS, Mallya A, Keshavamurthy R, Poonawala A

Journal: Neurology and Urodynamics Journal, accepted in June 2017, NAU-17-0131

Details: A follow-up article proving the concept of using a nasogastric tube as an abdominal pressure sensor in urodynamics, contributing to novel diagnostic methods in urology.
