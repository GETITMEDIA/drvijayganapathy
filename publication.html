J Clin Diagn Res · July 2016

Penile circular fasciocutaneous (McAninch) flap as an option for complex anterior urethral stricture

Authors: Vijay Ganapathy S, Karthikeyan VS, Mallya A, Vishwanath K, Sreenivas J, Keshavamurthy R

Journal: J Clin Diagn Res. 2016 Jul;10(7):PD09-11

Evaluates the penile circular fasciocutaneous (McAninch) flap as a viable surgical option for complex anterior urethral strictures when buccal mucosal grafts are unsuitable.

J Clin Diagn Res · July 2017

Page kidney in Wunderlich syndrome causing acute renal failure and urosepsis

Authors: Vijay Ganapathy S, Sreenivas J, Keshavamurthy R

Journal: J Clin Diagn Res. 2017 Jul;15(8):24731

A clinical case report detailing successful minimally invasive management of Page kidney in Wunderlich syndrome complicated by acute renal failure and urosepsis.

International Journal of Urology · July 2016

Nasogastric tube as abdominal pressure sensor in urodynamics

Authors: Ganapathy SV, Keshavamurthy R, Poonawala A, Mallya A, Karthikeyan VS

Journal: International Journal of Urology. 2016 Jul, Vol. 23 (Wiley-Blackwell)

Presents a novel, accessible method utilizing a nasogastric tube as an abdominal pressure sensor during urodynamic evaluation to enhance diagnostic accuracy.

Investigative and Clinical Urology · 2017

Validation of APACHE II scoring system at 24 hours after admission as a prognostic tool in urosepsis

Authors: Vijay Ganapathy S, Karthikeyan VS, Mallya A

Journal: Investigative and Clinical Urology (Accepted June 2017, ID: ICU-17-050)

Validates the APACHE II clinical scoring tool at 24 hours post-admission for predicting clinical outcomes and stratifying risk in urosepsis patients.

Neurourology and Urodynamics · 2017

Nasogastric tube as abdominal pressure sensor in urodynamics: Proof of concept

Authors: Vijay Ganapathy S, Karthikeyan VS, Mallya A, Keshavamurthy R, Poonawala A

Journal: Neurourology and Urodynamics Journal (Accepted June 2017, NAU-17-0131)

Follow-up proof-of-concept investigation confirming accuracy and clinical feasibility of nasogastric abdominal pressure sensing in urodynamic testing.
