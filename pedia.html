Birth Through Adolescence

Reassuring Care for Young Patients & Parents

Paediatric urology cares for boys and girls from infancy through adolescence, addressing structural developmental variations, urinary infections, bladder control issues, and kidney conditions.

With fine paediatric endoscopes and gentle clinical protocols, Dr. Vijay Ganapathy helps safeguard lifelong kidney function while ensuring parents are clearly guided through every stage of care.

Conditions Managed

Common Paediatric Urological Conditions Treated

Congenital & Structural Care

  • Undescended testes (cryptorchidism)
  • Hypospadias (urethral opening repair)
  • Epispadias & chordee correction
  • Congenital genitourinary malformations
  • Phimosis management

Kidney & Bladder Function

  • Antenatal hydronephrosis (swelling detected in utero)
  • Urinary obstruction & vesicoureteral reflux (VUR)
  • Bedwetting & daytime urinary incontinence
  • Neurogenic bladder (e.g., spina bifida)

Stones, Trauma & Oncology

  • Paediatric urolithiasis (kidney & bladder stones)
  • Repair of genitourinary trauma
  • Kidney tumors (e.g., Wilms tumor evaluation)
  • Paediatric-size endoscopic stone removal
Parent Support

When Should Parents Seek Evaluation?

Early screening—especially for antenatal hydronephrosis detected during pregnancy ultrasound, recurrent urinary tract infections, or undescended testes—allows timely, gentle intervention that protects future renal growth.
